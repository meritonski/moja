Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases or subscription? +

Go to your Profile settings inside the MojaMatch app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are logged into the Apple ID account you used to make the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? +

Subscriptions are managed directly through your Apple ID:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions.
  3. Select MojaMatch and tap Cancel Subscription.
How do I delete my account or personal data? +

You can delete your account inside the app by opening Settings > Account > Delete Account. Alternatively, send a request to mojamatch@gmail.com with your registered username.

The app is crashing or freezing. What should I do? +

Try the following troubleshooting steps:

  • Force restart the MojaMatch app.
  • Ensure you have updated to the latest version in the App Store.
  • Restart your iPhone.
  • If the issue persists, reach out to us with your device model and iOS version.
