Child Safety Standards

Developer: Meriton Gjoshi · Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Scope and adult-only access

These standards apply to Moja, including its Android and iOS apps, profiles, photos, messages and community features. Moja is for adults aged 18 and over. People under 18 may not create or use an account. Suspected underage accounts can be reported for review and will be removed if the user is found to be under 18.

2. Prohibition of child sexual abuse and exploitation

Moja explicitly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). For these standards, a child is anyone under 18. Content or conduct that sexually abuses, exploits or endangers a child is prohibited throughout Moja, including private messages.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Creating, uploading, possessing for distribution, requesting, sharing or promoting child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or linking to such material.
  • Sexual images or videos involving minors, including computer-generated, AI-generated or manipulated depictions of child sexual abuse.
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, sexual conversations targeting minors, or arranging contact with a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual extortion, threats or coercion involving minors, including demands for intimate images or money.
  • Child sex trafficking, commercial sexual exploitation of children, or facilitating any of these activities.

3. Reporting a concern

Use Moja's in-app reporting function to report a suspicious profile, message or other content. You can also contact mojamatch@gmail.com with the subject Child safety report.

Include the relevant username or account identifier, the approximate date and a brief description. Do not download, copy, forward or email suspected CSAM, and do not ask anyone to provide additional abusive material. Report the location of the content instead. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

4. Review and enforcement

Moja treats child safety reports as a priority. We review reports and take appropriate action when we become aware of prohibited content or conduct, including removing or disabling access to CSAM and other violating content, and suspending or terminating accounts responsible for CSAE.

Relevant information is handled securely and preserved where necessary for applicable legal obligations or lawful requests. We report confirmed CSAM to the competent regional authority or NCMEC, as applicable, in accordance with applicable law, and cooperate with lawful requests from the relevant authorities.
